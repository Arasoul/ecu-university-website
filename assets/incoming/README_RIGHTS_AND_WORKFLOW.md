# ECU visual asset research pack — 21 October 2026

This is a SOURCE/APPROVAL package, not a licensed photo redistribution bundle. It has verified/unverified candidates and canonical source pages for campus exteriors, faculty/staff events, students, research, gallery and news imagery. It intentionally contains NO copied third-party photographs, because their reuse rights have not been established. Images published on ecu.edu.eg are not automatically licensed for republishing on an independently hosted site.

### To obtain the actual deployable ZIP
1. Confirm with ECU communications/media office that you have permission to reuse the photos and logo for the replacement university website, including any photographer attribution requirements and identifiable-person consent policy.
2. Mark approved_for_publication=true and record a rights reference for every selected row.
3. Get original full-resolution files if ECU has them; do not scrape thumbnails when originals are available.
4. Download those approved originals into public/media/... grouped by category. Optimize into WebP/AVIF and preserve source credit in JSON.
5. Keep one accurate branch mapping: Nasr City opening photo from official page; LinkedIn older building photo must NOT be assigned Heliopolis until confirmed.

### Suggested visual asset quota
Homepage 3-5 real photos; Campus branches 3 per branch; Faculties 1 hero each; News and events 12-20; Staff and leadership 6-10 verified portraits; Student life 10; Research 6; Careers/partners 3; About page 3. Original total target around 60-85 rights-cleared distinct images. This is a design target, NOT a claim of asset availability.

### Source/use boundary
External original ECU pages may remain cited as editorial sources, but the public website should be the principal navigation destination: no 'Back to old official website' call-to-action, no old-brand homepage link. Verified protected third-party services such as the current admissions application portal should remain external when replacement functionality has not been authorized.
